05 / FROM OBSERVATION TO DECISION

Bring the evidence into your crop plan.

Combine climate history with soil-test values, previous crops, water availability and locally reviewed crop rules. Satellite imagery alone does not choose a crop.

✓
Evidence integrity is built into KISAN-X.Every layer must show its source, date, spatial scale, units and limitations. NASA POWER is a gridded baseline; GPM is a rainfall estimate; MODIS is landscape context; soil nutrients require a verified Soil Health Card or laboratory report. EVIDENCE LEDGER
Three kinds of information
M
Measured
Lab soil tests, field measurements and verified farmer observations.
S
Observed remotely
Satellite layer date, product, resolution, and image-quality metadata when available. Derived indices appear only after a pixel-analysis workflow is connected.
P
Predicted / inferred
Model output with model version, uncertainty and validation coverage.

VALIDATION GATE
Before any advisory goes live
